How it works: the video plays here and this page records what you hear, for as long as your video runs. When the browser asks what to share, pick this tab and tick Share tab audio. Works in Chrome and Edge on a computer. If an ad plays first, let it finish and press Record again.

This browser can't record a tab's sound. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer, or the Windows app, which does the whole job from the link: download YouTubeSoundOnVideo.exe.
